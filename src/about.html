About Limited Audience

Welcome to Limited Audience, Fashionable Bondage. This site collects material that was shot over one and a half decades. All of it was rigged, shot, and lovingly edited by myself, Frank. It features both professional and amateur models, but all of them were a joy to work with, and I'm forever proud of the amazing work we've done together.

The trailers generally contain links to places you can purchase the full video. You have my and my models' gratitude for each and every purchase!

What started as a bondage tribute site in 2000, grew to one of the world's oldest bondage sites. Since the beginning it has featured original and unique content. It has gone through a multitude of redesigns over that time, and this is its latest incarnation.

Be sure to check back often—there are 100 random photos on offer each day!
